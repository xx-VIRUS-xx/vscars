FAQ

Common questions

Does my laptop need to be on?
Yes — VSCARS is a bridge, not a cloud VM. Your laptop stays on, and your phone connects through a secure Ngrok HTTPS tunnel. Think of it as a remote control for your actual machine, not a replacement.
I already pay for Copilot. What am I paying for?
VSCARS is the mobile interface and infrastructure layer. Your GitHub Copilot subscription key stays yours — we never touch it. You're paying for the thing that lets you use Copilot from your phone at 10pm.
Is it safe to expose my terminal?
JWT authentication, rate limiting, a command audit log, and HTTPS via Ngrok's encrypted tunnel. The self-hosted plan keeps everything on your own network — nothing routes through our servers. You stay in control.
Can I install it like an app?
Yes. VSCARS is a PWA. Open it in mobile Safari or Chrome, tap "Add to Home Screen," and it launches like a native app — full-screen, offline-capable splash, the whole thing.
What if I only want to use it solo?
Pro plan is built for solo devs. Team is for when you want to give a collaborator or contractor access without handing them SSH credentials. The self-hosted plan works for either.
